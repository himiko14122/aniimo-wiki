The **Aniimo full creature list** is gated by three overlapping conditions: time of day, weather state, and phenomena events. Phenomena are rare server-wide events announced in advance through the in-game Pathfinder board, and they typically unlock a Mythical-tier spawn window for 30 minutes.

### Twine Fusion and Mega Evolution

Twine is the headline mechanic that lets you fuse two compatible Aniimo into a hybrid form with a custom ability pool. The [Aniimo evolution guide](/en/evolution/evolution-guide/) covers the full Twine tree, but the key takeaway is that Twine forms can inherit rarity from either parent, which is the main reason **Aniimo mythical creatures** are so sought-after: a Mythical parent guarantees a Mythical-or-better child.

Mega Evolution is the final evolution layer, available only to creatures that reach the Mythical bracket. A Mega Evolved Aniimo gains a unique Z-Move-style ultimate ability and a stat boost of roughly 30% across the board, which is enough to swing mirror matchups in competitive play.
