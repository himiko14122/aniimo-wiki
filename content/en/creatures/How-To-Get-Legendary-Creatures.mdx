## Catching Mechanics and Chain Bonuses

### How Aniipod Timing Works

Catching an Aniimo requires you to throw an Aniipod while the creature is in a calm state, which the game signals with a small ring beneath the creature's feet. The ring shrinks during the calm window, and a perfect throw within the smallest ring grants a critical catch bonus that boosts your registration progress. Critical catches on rare or higher creatures are the fastest way to fill the [Aniimo creature collection guide](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4126040/Aniimo/) entries, because each crit fills an extra completion tick on top of the standard registration.

| Catch Quality | Ring Size Window | Registration Bonus | Aniipod Cost |
|---------------|------------------|--------------------|--------------|
| Standard | Full ring | 1 tick | 1 |
| Good | Mid ring | 2 ticks | 1 |
| Critical | Inner ring | 4 ticks | 1 |
| Flawless | Center pulse | 6 ticks | 2 (double throw) |

Flawless catches cost two Aniipods but award six registration ticks, which is the highest yield per encounter in the game. Most legendary hunts should budget around 30 to 40 Aniipods per run, with at least 10 reserved for chain bonus activations later in the session.

### Chain Catch Bonus and Legendary Triggers

Chains activate when you catch three or more creatures in a row without fleeing, failing, or letting more than 60 seconds pass between throws. At chain levels 5, 10, and 15, the game rolls a hidden bonus that can upgrade the next rare encounter into a guaranteed legendary spawn within the same biome. According to community testing shared on the official Aniimo YouTube channel, chain level 10 is the practical sweet spot because the chance of a legendary trigger climbs above 40% while the chain remains manageable to maintain.

Players who want to [Aniimo how to get rare creatures](https://www.aniimo.com/) consistently should treat chain bonuses as the primary tool, not weather or luck. The chain does not need to include the legendary itself; it just needs to be active when you reach the legendary zone.
