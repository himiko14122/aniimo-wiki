## What the Aniimo Press Release Archive Covers

The Pawprint Studio communication pipeline produces several distinct document types. Understanding the difference helps players interpret the urgency and scope of each statement correctly. The **press release** tier is reserved for platform-level news: launch dates, new platforms, monetization changes, or major partnerships. Below that, the **developer update** tier handles mid-development milestones such as closed beta phases and feature reveals. The **community update** tier is the most frequent — short, conversational notes addressing player feedback, known issues, and event timing.

Aniimo press release documents typically appear on three channels simultaneously: the [Aniimo on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4126040/Aniimo/) news hub, the [official Aniimo website](https://www.aniimo.com/) announcements page, and pinned posts in the Discord server. Cross-referencing all three is the recommended habit because the Steam version sometimes leads platform-specific details while Discord leads community-facing contest winners and event codes.

| Document Type | Release Cadence | Typical Content | Primary Channel |
|---------------|-----------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Press Release | Monthly or at milestones | Launch dates, partnerships, platform reveals | Steam News + Official Site |

| Community Update | Weekly | Event timing, contest winners, minor patches | Discord + X (Twitter) |
| Day One Patch Notes | Single release | Day-one bug fixes, balance tuning | Steam News + In-game mail |
