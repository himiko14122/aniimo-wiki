## Aniimo Balance Changes and Combat Tweaks in the New Update

Combat is one of the systems the developers iterated on heavily during the closed betas, and the Aniimo new update delivers the most refined version yet. The patch notes focus on three areas: **Aniipod catch timing**, **Twine ability cooldowns**, and **Egg Heist scoring**. Each area saw measurable adjustments based on telemetry from the second beta.

| System | Previous Behavior | New Behavior in Patch | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aniipod catch window | Strict timing, harsh fail state | Generous 0.4s buffer added | Friendlier to new players |
| Twine ability cooldowns | Long base cooldowns | Reduced by ~20% on most skills | Faster combat flow |
| Egg Heist scoring | Fixed multipliers | Dynamic multipliers by round | Rewards late-round plays |
| Brute Enrage Timer | 90 seconds | 75 seconds | Tighter boss pacing |
| Status effect stacking | Capped at 2 | Capped at 3 | More build variety |

The **Brute Enrage Timer** change is particularly notable because it changes the rhythm of every boss encounter in the late game. Players who learned a 90-second rotation in the beta will need to adjust, and the patch notes specifically call this out as a difficulty-curve fix rather than a nerf.

### Why the Catch Window Matters

The catch timing change is the most discussed item in the Aniimo patch notes among community testers, because the previous strict window was the single biggest source of failed encounters in the second beta. Adding a 0.4-second buffer makes the mechanic forgiving without making it trivial, and it aligns with how most creature-catching games handle input windows.
