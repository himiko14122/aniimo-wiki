## Aniimo Changelog Reading Guide

Reading an Aniimo changelog effectively is a skill that pays off the moment a new build goes live, because the patch text is dense and the patch often bundles 30 to 50 discrete changes into a single document. The studio uses a consistent four-section structure for each Aniimo update, and learning that structure lets you skim to the parts that matter to your current build.

### Changelog Section Breakdown

| Section | What It Covers | Why It Matters |
|---------|----------------|----------------|
| New Content | Biomes, Aniimo, events | Pulls players back into the game |
| Balance | Damage, stamina, capture rates | Affects every active build |
| Bug Fixes | Quest blockers, crashes, soft locks | Determines whether your save is safe |
| Known Issues | Unresolved problems | Sets expectations for the next hotfix |

The balance section is where the studio is most transparent about internal playtest data, and it is the section that competitive players read first. Bug fix entries are usually grouped by platform, which means a PC-only fix will not affect console players and vice versa. Known issues are not promised a fix timeline, so the absence of a known issue from a future Aniimo changelog does not necessarily mean it was silently resolved.
