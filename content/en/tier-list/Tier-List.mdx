## S Tier: The Definitive Carry Picks

S tier creatures win matches on their own. Each one can swing an Egg Heist round, solo a world boss, or carry a co-op raid when played to its ceiling. For example, a fully-bonded Mythic-tier creature with maxed affection and its signature skill rotation can out-DPS a full party of A-tier picks in Phase 2 of the Stormpeak world boss, while also tanking the enrage cleave thanks to its native 15% damage reduction passive — a stat threshold no lower-tier creature can replicate even with identical gear.

### Featured S-Tier Creatures

- **Lunara** — A celestial fox-line Aniimo with the highest burst magic scaling in the game. Her signature move **Star Cascade** deals AoE damage that scales with Twine bond level, making her the single most efficient boss-melter on the roster.

- **Volkraken** — A volcanic cephalopod that combines lava zone control with self-healing. Volkraken is the **best creature for PvP** in 1v1 matchups because its **Magma Shell** passive reflects a portion of incoming damage.

- **Aetherwing** — The fastest flyer in the Aniimo roster. Aetherwing is unmatched in open-world traversal and in aerial objective capture during Egg Heist, where vertical reach decides most chokepoints.
